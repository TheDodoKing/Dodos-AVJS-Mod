Gamerules (True by default):
dodosAVJSModExecutionerGuardFix - Changes the check on executioner sword for the logic to not run from guard amplifier 1 (holding crouch) to amplifier 2 (perfect guard), so now you cant just hold sneak to survive death penalty

dodosAVJSModISOHBlackropeDamageFix - Damage of skills while holding isoh and black rope will be normal

dodosAVJSModDagonExtFix: stops clone spawning of dagons extension, various other buffs to dagon in water including saturation, regen 4, infinite oxygen and dolphins grace (default: true)

dodosAVJSModStunDamageThreshold1: modifies the first threshold of damage which defines what percentage of somethings max hp as damage must be done to apply 10 ticks of stun (default: 5 which is 5 percent of max hp)

dodosAVJSModStunDamageThreshold2: modifies the second threshold of damage which defines what percentage of max hp as damage must be done to apply slowness (duration of 75 * percentage of max hp damage ticks, max of 60 ticks, amplifier of 5 * percentage of max hp damage) and applies cooldown of the same duration as slowness but always amplifier 0 (default: 10 which is 10 percent of max hp)

dodosAVJSModStunDamageThreshold3: modifies the third threshold of damage which controls the percentage of max hp of damage you need to do to apply more stun effect (follows the same formula as the slowness of threshold 2, percentage of max hp damage * 75 ticks duration however always has an amplifier of 1) and if the enemy's domain has a duration of less than 600 ticks (30 seconds) it will be canceled
